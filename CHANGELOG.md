## 🚀 [v1.0.0] - Official Release 🔴(MOST RECENT!)🔴

Welcome to the official release of the **Goofy Chaos Panel**! This bookmarklet/console script turns any webpage into an interactive chaos playground with real-time text editing, goofy page effects, and visual overlays.

### ✨ Features Included in v1.0.0

#### 1. Live Editing & Stealth
* ✏️ **Live Text Editing:** Toggle `document.designMode` to click and edit any text directly on the page.
* 🏫 **School Mode (Fake Calculator):** Instantly hides the panel and overlays a functional math calculator for quick disguise.
* 🗣 **Text-to-Speech:** Enter custom text to have the browser speak it out loud.

#### 2. Visual Overlays & Filters
* 💥 **Nuke Explosion:** Triggers an explosion sound, screen shake, white flash, and red fallout tint.
* 🟢 **Matrix Rain Overlay:** Renders green digital matrix rain across the entire screen.
* 🌓 **Invert Colors:** Swaps the webpage color spectrum to its exact opposite.
* 🪩 **Party Disco Flash:** Flashes the background rapidly through random colors.
* ⬛ **Redacted Mode:** Blurs and censors all page text into solid black blocks.

#### 3. Ultra Goofy Chaos
* 🌐 **Gravity Collapse:** Drops all webpage elements to the bottom of the screen with random rotations.
* 👀 **Googly Eyes:** Pins interactive eyes to the screen that follow your mouse cursor.
* 📀 **Bouncing DVD Panel:** Makes the control panel bounce off the screen boundaries.
* ✏️ **Comic Sans Overhaul:** Forces Comic Sans font across the entire site.
* 🙃 **Upside Down Page:** Rotates the page 180 degrees.
* 🌀 **Spinning Elements:** Makes all page elements spin in continuous circles.
* 🫨 **Earthquake Shake:** Rapidly shakes the entire webpage body.
* ✨ **Emoji Cursor Trail:** Spawns animated floating emojis where your cursor moves.
* 🐱 **Cat Image Swapper:** Replaces every image on the site with random cat pictures.
* 🎊 **Confetti Cannon:** Fires 50 colorful confetti particles down the screen.

#### 4. Screen Canvas & Drawing
* ✏️ **Screen Drawing Mode:** Enables a transparent cyan drawing overlay across the viewport.
* 🧼 **Clear Canvas:** Wipes all screen drawings clean.
